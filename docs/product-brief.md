# Product Brief

## Thesis

STRATIS Healthcare Ops turns discharge workflow noise into an auditable operational decision loop.

## Target User

Healthcare operations leaders, discharge coordinators, bed managers, and transformation teams evaluating AI-enabled operational governance patterns.

## Core Workflow

1. Load synthetic discharge cases.
2. Classify blockers.
3. Score SLA and bed-release risk.
4. Generate owner-specific recommendation.
5. Record human review.
6. Preserve governance event.
7. Produce weekly executive brief.

## Briefing Modes

Operating briefs can be generated for Daily, Weekly, or Monthly use. Executive mode keeps the brief concise for leadership review. Detailed mode includes supporting evidence, recommendation detail, and recent governance events.

Daily briefs use current-period synthetic cases. Weekly briefs include Daily and Weekly synthetic cases. Monthly briefs include the full synthetic operating sample.

## Differentiation

The MVP foregrounds auditability and human decision design rather than presenting AI as an autonomous clinical system.
